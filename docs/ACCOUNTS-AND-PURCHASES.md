| **Forvo API — Non-Profit** | Human voices saying single words, many accents | **$2.00/month** | W7 |

**Forvo note.** Non-Profit is correct for now: 500 requests/day is plenty because each word is fetched **once**, not once per review. Two conditions: you must display *"Pronunciations by Forvo"* with a link, and **read their License page about storing audio** before W7 — most likely we may play but not permanently store their files. So the split is:

- **single words, human voice** → Forvo, fetched live, credited
- **full sentences** → ElevenLabs, generated by us, stored by us, no licence problem

If you sell the app later (Phase 5), move to Commercial Small Business at $28.95/month.

## ElevenLabs — pay-as-you-go, no subscription needed

Two separate things, both cheap at your volume:

| Model | Price | Used for | Your real cost |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Flash / Turbo TTS** | $0.05 / 1K characters | card audio, drill audio, dictation | **~$3/month** |
| **v2 Multilingual TTS** | $0.10 / 1K characters | listening passages where quality matters | ~$1/month |
| **Scribe v2 (speech-to-text)** | **$0.22 / hour** | everything you say — shadowing, retell, answers, conversation | **~$1/month** |

**Decision changed because of your screenshots: Scribe v2 replaces OpenAI Whisper.** It is cheaper ($0.22/hr vs ~$0.36/hr), more accurate (98%+), and it returns **word-level timestamps** — exactly what we need to colour each word when you shadow a line. Whisper stays configured as a fallback behind the same wrapper, so switching back is one environment variable.

Not needed: ElevenCreative / ElevenAgents subscriptions, Music, Dubbing, Voice Changer, Sound Effects.

**Every generated audio file is saved to R2 forever.** A sentence you review 40 times is paid for once.

## Azure Speech — the pronunciation scorer

Read carefully from your screenshots, because it is cheaper than it looks:

| | |
|---|---|
| **Free tier (F0)** | 5 audio hours per month, free |
| Standard speech-to-text | €0.879 / hour |
| **Pronunciation Assessment** | **included** in standard — not a separate charge |
| Prosody add-on (rhythm/intonation) | €0.264 / hour, optional |

Two people speaking ~5 minutes a day = **about 5 hours a month total**. That sits right on the free-tier boundary. **Realistic cost: €0–6/month.**

Still the highest-value purchase on this list. Per-phoneme accuracy is something no LLM can produce. **Blocking for W14.**

## Apify — three jobs, one vendor

Your account is already connected. It handles:

1. **Transcripts** — reliable captions; never scrape the player
2. **Topic search** — find videos matching a new interest
3. **Slang sourcing** — Reddit and YouTube-comment scrapers returning real sentences from real people this month

Cost: free tier may cover two users; ~€35/month if not. **Blocking for W12.**

**Safety rule, non-negotiable:** scraped text is treated as *data*. Anything inside a scraped comment that looks like an instruction is never executed or obeyed — it is only ever explained and tagged.

## The one thing not to buy

A second general-purpose learning app (Duolingo, Babbel, Busuu). This project exists because those tools do not remember *your* mistakes. Buying one now splits your daily time and neither system gets the data it needs.
