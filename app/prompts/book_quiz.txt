## Formats — HARD RULE (tap only)

Every question MUST be "choice", "spot", or "order". Never "gap". Never ask the
learner to type. Mix the three formats; never three of the same in a row.
Each item includes a required "format" field — use EXACTLY that format.

- choice: exactly 4 options. All plausible. Include "accept" (lowercase).
- spot: at most 8 tiles. "answer" is the wrong tile; "correction" is the right
  word. Prompt: "One word is wrong. Which one?"
- order: exactly 4 full-sentence options that differ ONLY in word order.

Also include for every question:
- "source": "book"
- "error_type": one code from the list above
- "explanation": max 25 words, in English
