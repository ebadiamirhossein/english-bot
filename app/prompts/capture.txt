language patterns — do not treat this as error correction.

Output MUST be a single JSON object with this exact shape:

{{
  "ok": true,
  "explanation": "what the passage means, plus anything opaque for a B1/B2 learner (idioms, phrasal verbs, register, business or legal convention) — max 25 words",
  "track": "work",
  "chunks": [
    {{
      "chunk": "multi-word phrase worth keeping",
      "full_sentence": "a sentence that contains the chunk",
      "meaning": "brief gloss in plain English"
    }}
  ]
}}

If the passage is empty, not English, or has nothing useful to mine, return:
{{ "ok": false, "explanation": "", "track": null, "chunks": [] }}

Chunk count (adaptive — never pad):
- aim for about one chunk per ~40 words of the passage
- minimum 1 when ok=true and something is worth keeping; maximum 5
- a two-line Slack message should yield 1–2 chunks, not 5
- prefer fewer high-value chunks over padding

Rules for each chunk:
- multi-word phrases worth reusing (not single vocabulary words)
- each chunk MUST appear inside its full_sentence (same words; capitalisation
  may differ where a chunk opens a sentence)
- meaning is a short plain-English gloss

Privacy — critical:
- Prefer a GENERIC carrier sentence that still contains the chunk and teaches
  the pattern, over the verbatim source sentence, whenever the pattern
  survives the substitution.
- Names, addresses, amounts, company identifiers, and emails must NOT appear
  in chunk or full_sentence when a neutral carrier teaches the same thing.
- Example: source "Please send the Q3 forecast to Mira Chen by Friday (€42k)."
  → chunk "by Friday", full_sentence "Please send the forecast by Friday."
  (not the name or the figure)

track: one of "work", "life", "curiosity", or null if unsure. Do not guess.

Reply with only the JSON object, no markdown fences or commentary.
